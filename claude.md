# KidDinoDress project context

Last updated: 2026-11-13 (Asia/Karachi).

## Business and objective

- Etsy shop: https://www.etsy.com/shop/KidDinoDress
- Sell original dinosaur daytime shirts for children, using Printify for production and fulfillment.
- Owner confirmed the legal seller account is registered in Pakistan; initial customer market is the United States.
- Long-term goal: a complete, sustainable e-commerce business. Immediate goal: recover selling and fulfillment, then validate a small collection profitably.
- The repository started with only a README. No standalone website or commerce implementation has been built.

## Latest owner decisions

- Use the lean approach. The proposed $290 launch allocation was rejected and is superseded.
- Current authorized spending is $0. No purchases, paid renewals, advertising, subscriptions or paid website tools for now.
- Start by improving one to three existing designs/listing drafts using free tools and artwork with verified rights.
- Check actual production, US shipping, tax and platform costs before choosing prices or reopening.
- Quote one sample for review; no sample purchase is authorized yet.
- Determine fulfillment cash needs from actual costs and affordable order quantities, rather than imposing an arbitrary reserve.
- Validate on Etsy first; defer a standalone website and paid acquisition.

## Verified audit observations

These were observed in authenticated Etsy and Printify browser sessions on November 13, 2026. They are historical observations, not a live health check; recheck before acting.

### Etsy

- Shop Manager said selling was blocked because Payoneer could not verify the owner's information. Etsy imposed holiday mode.
- Public storefront showed 0 sales and no reviews.
- Listings: 0 active, 0 drafts, 4 expired, 0 sold out and 0 inactive.
- All four listings expired January 2, 2026:
  - Toddler Dino Love Tee: $20.
  - Nap-o-saurus Rex toddler tee: $15.
  - Pop-o-saurus dinosaur bubble gum toddler tee: $15.
  - Baby Dinosaur T-Shirt with balloons: $19.99; title contained "Pixar Style" and variants included an unavailable SKU.
- Sleepwear-related tags appeared on daytime T-shirt listings; review intended use and wording.
- Four listings had no videos and showed 30-day returns/exchanges.
- Etsy listed Printify as connected on September 2, 2025. This historical authorization did not establish a working connection.
- Public shop location displayed United States, although the owner confirmed Pakistan seller registration. Review truthful seller and production location details.

### Printify

- Store status explicitly reported "Your Etsy connection expired," blocking publishing, order sync and fulfillment.
- Selected store was named KidDinoDress, but My Products was empty and Visit store pointed to an older DinoDance Etsy URL. Cause unknown: investigate before assuming a wrong account or creating duplicates.
- Standard order submission was automatic after 24 hours; personalized orders were manual.
- EU selling was enabled despite the agreed US-first scope; review destinations before reopening.
