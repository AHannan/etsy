# Etsy and Printify operations

## What must be verified before reactivation?

### Takeaway
A public URL cannot establish integration, payment health or readiness. Inspect seller dashboards before accepting orders.

### Cited Findings
- Connect through Printify's store menu > Manage my stores > Connect > Etsy, then Etsy authorization. One Etsy store connects to only one Printify account. [Connection guide](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483617508241-How-can-I-connect-my-Etsy-shop-to-Printify)
- Etsy distinguishes expired, deactivated, sold out, policy-removed and suspended listings. [Inactive listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040986253-Why-Is-My-Listing-Inactive)
- A voluntarily closed shop can be reopened on desktop; suspension needs separate resolution. [Reopen](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115015777088-How-to-Close-Your-Etsy-Shop)
- Deposits may be affected by reserves, invalid details or seller verification. Payoneer sellers currently receive Etsy deposits each Monday. [Deposits](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002080688-Why-Hasn-t-My-Money-Been-Deposited-Yet)

### Inferences
Checklist, not account observations: Dashboard warnings; Vacation Mode; listing status counts; unpaid balance; overdue orders/cases/messages; payout and identity verification; registered seller/bank country; Printify store identity; product and variant mapping to Etsy IDs; stock; current production/shipping costs; approval setting; payment method; ship-from and destination estimates. Start with manual approval during audit. A direct Printify sample checks quality/delivery but does not prove Etsy order import. Inspect a legitimate next order through import, approval, production, tracking sync and payout; avoid artificial self-purchases/reviews.

### Gaps
Private dashboards, seller country, warnings, integration and order history are unverified. No activation performed.

## How do fulfillment and service work?

### Takeaway
Printify charges the merchant separately from Etsy payouts. Reserve working capital and handle exceptions daily.

### Cited Findings
- Printify cannot withdraw Etsy revenue; it separately charges production/shipping from balance or linked payment method. Billing and Transactions shows costs/taxes. [Payment process](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483601124113-How-does-the-payment-process-work)
- Orders follow approval settings, enter production after payment, and shipped tracking syncs to the channel. [Order flow](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483638068241-What-happens-when-an-order-comes-in)
- Eligible damage/manufacturing claims require reporting with evidence within 30 calendar days of delivery. Wrong customer-selected size/color and change-of-mind returns are unsupported by Printify. [Returns](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483630299025-How-does-Printify-handle-refunds-and-returns)

### Inferences
Daily: messages, personalization approval, payment failures, delayed production/tracking. Weekly: reconcile Etsy revenue/fees with Printify charges, refunds and bank receipts. Publish exact garment sizing and return terms consistent with applicable consumer law; supplier terms do not automatically override consumer rights. Verify delivery performance before birthday promises. Illustrative working capital: 20 orders awaiting payout at assumed $14.50 fulfillment require $290 before replacement/refund contingency.

### Gaps
Provider performance, destination shipping, approval settings, taxes and governing consumer rights unknown.

## What are fees and viable unit economics?

### Takeaway
Use seller-country fees and delivered cost for each variant; garment markup alone can hide losses.

### Cited Findings
- Listing/renewal is $0.20; transaction fee is 6.5% including charged shipping/gift wrap; additional processing/tax charges apply. [Etsy fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy)
- Bank location determines processing: US 3% + $0.25; Pakistan 6.5% + $0.30. Processing includes shipping and applicable sales tax. [Processing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy)
- Attributed Offsite Ads orders carry 15% below $10,000 trailing-365-day Etsy sales or 12% at/above threshold, capped at $100/order. Participation differs by threshold. [Offsite Ads](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367-How-Etsy-s-Offsite-Ads-Work)

### Inferences
Illustration only, not actual quotes or forecast: $24.99 item with free shipping; assumed $10 production, $4.50 shipping, $0.75 replacement allowance, $1.62 transaction fee, $0.20 listing allocation. US processing $1.00 leaves $6.92 contribution before ads, taxes, overhead and labor (27.7%). Pakistan processing $1.92 leaves $6.00 (24.0%). A 15% attributed Offsite Ad costs $3.75, leaving $3.17/$2.25. Supplier sales tax, processing on buyer tax, VAT on fees, FX, Payoneer, discounts, subscriptions and labor omitted; add where applicable. Before overhead, breakeven acquisition cost is at most $6.92/$6.00; target must be lower. Attribute actual ads costs and avoid universally double-counting Etsy Ads with Offsite Ads. Scale contribution, not revenue.

### Gaps
Timezone is not evidence of seller registration. Actual country, provider quotes, mix, taxes and acquisition costs unverified.

## What policies and product documents matter?

### Takeaway
Disclose production partners and secure documentation for the actual finished children's apparel in the target market.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy permits Printify-like partners for seller-designed products and requires disclosure on applicable listings. [Partners](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000336547-Working-with-Production-Partners-on-Etsy)
- CPSC advises obtaining/reviewing supplier CPC/GCC documentation; manufacturers/importers issue certificates for applicable children's rules. Retailers have unsafe/noncompliant product duties. [Retailers](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/Business-Guidance/Retailers-Product-Safety-and-Your-Responsibilities)
- Children's tracking marks must be legible/permanent and on product/packaging to extent practicable, identifying source, production location/date and batch/run. [Labels](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/tracking-label)
- CPSC apparel guidance covers flammability, lead, certificates, tracking and additional sleepwear rules. [Apparel guide](https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/Guide-to-US-Apparel-and-Household-Textiles.pdf)

### Inferences
For US launch, ask provider for documents covering actual blank plus applied ink/printing and tracking arrangements; blank-only documentation may not resolve finished-product obligations. Record SKU/provider, documents and artwork rights. Begin with ordinary daytime tees; assess additional rules before sleepwear claims. Original dinosaur illustrations avoid dependence on movie/cartoon character licenses. Assess EU/UK separately if serving those destinations.

### Gaps
No garment/provider/ink documents reviewed. Ages, markets and importer/manufacturer role unknown. Checklist does not establish compliance or noncompliance.
