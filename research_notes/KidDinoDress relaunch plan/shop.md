## What should the listing and brand audit check once accessible?

### Takeaway
Audit buyer confidence and the entire listing rather than filling titles with repeated keywords. The actions below are proposed checks, not observed defects in KidDinoDress.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy recommends clear titles naming the item, leading with important characteristics, avoiding repetitive or subjective words and considering fewer than 15 words. Its optional title tool is a starting point, and it recommends exporting listing CSVs before changes. [Title guidance](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697)
- Etsy looks across titles, tags, attributes, descriptions, photos and reviews. Use all 13 relevant tags and specific categories and attributes. Ads and Etsy Plus do not improve organic placement. Repeated renewals for a small recency boost are not a sound optimization strategy. [How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/how-etsy-search-works/375461474487)
- Etsy's visibility dashboard highlights listing, shop and customer-service improvements. It recommends clear, well-lit primary images, ideally at least 2000 pixels in both dimensions, a single finished-product primary image rather than a collage, specific categories and a return policy. Shop completion and service quality also matter. [Search visibility guidance](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/25869947521175-How-to-Use-the-Etsy-Search-Visibility-Page)

### Inferences
Proposed review checklist:
- Define buyer and use: adults buying dinosaur apparel for children, with precise garment size ranges rather than vague age claims.
- Position a small coherent range: everyday dinosaur interest; birthday name/age option if reliably supported; dinosaur styles across color preferences. These are hypotheses needing demand validation.
- Prefer original recognizable dinosaur characters, coherent typography and consistent thumbnails across the range.
- Check that imagery accurately represents the actual blank, print dimensions and selected color. Include actual sample photography, print close-ups, garment measurements, fit guidance, color options, care guidance and honest production/delivery information.
- Draft example title only after confirming garment facts: “Personalized Dinosaur Birthday T Shirt, Toddler Name and Age.” Do not claim cotton, softness, organic status or safety certifications without documentation.
- Check banner, icon, About story, production-partner disclosure, FAQs, size guidance and return/cancellation policy.
- Prioritize the highest historical potential listings using actual stats, not guesses. Export baseline data before edits and record changes so results can be interpreted.

### Gaps
- No defensible shop-specific SEO score, photo critique or rewrite can be supplied until actual listings are available.
- No keyword volumes, competitor revenue or conversion benchmarks were verified in this subtask. Suggested niches are experiments, not demand estimates.

### Takeaway
Resolve account and fulfillment readiness before generating traffic. Historical performance and contribution margin should determine what to retain and scale.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy's search guide notes that after vacation mode the platform needs fresh buyer-interaction evidence and traffic may differ from the pre-vacation period. [How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/how-etsy-search-works/375461474487)

### Inferences
Minimum authenticated audit evidence:
1. Shop dashboard notices, vacation mode, billing balance, verification and payout readiness; count active, inactive, expired and draft listings separately.
2. Open orders, unresolved messages, cases, refunds and customer-service statistics before a relaunch.
3. Prior twelve months and lifetime Etsy Stats, order/listing exports, fees, ad spend and sales by listing and destination; never expose buyer personal data in repository notes.
4. Printify shop connection status; each listing's Printify product and variant mapping; current blank/provider availability; production cost and shipping by target destination; manual versus automatic order approval; valid production-payment method.
5. One physical sample and a controlled end-to-end order test with explicit owner authorization for any purchase or spend.
6. Baseline record of price, shipping charge, landed production cost, fees, refunds and acquisition spend for each proposed hero listing.

### Gaps
