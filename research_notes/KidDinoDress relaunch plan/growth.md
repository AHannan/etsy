# KidDinoDress positioning and growth

## What should the product and listing strategy be?

### Takeaway
Treat positioning as a test: original dinosaur artwork for children, with a small coherent collection and clear garment information. There is no verified evidence yet that any particular dinosaur keyword or design will sell for this shop.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy considers multiple listing signals in query matching, and recommends using all 13 tags, relevant attributes, and specific categories. Ads do not directly improve organic placement. — [How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/375461474487)
- Current title guidance favors a clearly named item, important objective traits, fewer repetitions, and scannability; Etsy suggests considering titles under 15 words. — [Listing title guidance](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697)
- Etsy describes the initial photo as important for attracting clicks and recommends buyer-oriented listing details and supporting images to improve purchasing confidence. — [Creating Listings That Convert](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/366469719354)
- Marketplace Insights supplies actual Etsy search and listing counts for the past 30 days, related keywords, and up to 15 free keyword searches weekly. These are research inputs, not sales forecasts. — [Marketplace Insights](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/35122361353239-How-Do-I-Use-Etsy-s-Marketplace-Insights-Tool)

### Inferences
Recommendations, not externally established facts:
- Start with 6–12 distinct, original designs on one verified toddler garment and one verified youth garment, rather than hundreds of near duplicates.
- Test three creative directions: playful everyday dinosaurs; dinosaur birthday ages; educational species or fossil illustrations. Personalization is optional and should wait until proofing and fulfillment are reliable.
- Test inclusive colors and styling rather than assuming dinosaur apparel is only for boys. Keep actual garment size availability explicit; toddler and youth sizing are not interchangeable.
- Candidate keyword research phrases: dinosaur toddler shirt, kids dinosaur shirt, dinosaur birthday shirt, personalized dino shirt, T rex kids shirt, dinosaur girl shirt. These are hypotheses; none has verified search volume in this review.
- Example title only if all claims match the chosen product: "Kids Dinosaur T Shirt, Colorful T Rex Print, Toddler and Youth Sizes". Do not call a garment cotton, organic, soft, sustainable or US-made without support.
- Create an image sequence showing the design clearly, a real sample, size measurements, available colors, print close-up, care instructions, and production/delivery explanation. Do not fabricate customer reviews or misleading lifestyle images.
- Use a plain shop subtitle explaining "Dinosaur shirts for kids". The existing shop name can be assessed after account history and brand assets are understood; a rename is not needed merely to begin validating products.

### Gaps
- Actual historical bestsellers, conversion, search terms, garment models, artwork ownership, audience country, and keyword volumes remain unknown.
- No market size, competitor revenue or future sales claim is justified by this research.

## How should the first 90 days work?

### Takeaway
Sequence reactivation, quality and margin validation, a controlled launch, and only then increased spending. A calendar alone should never trigger scaling.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy Stats exposes visits, orders, revenue, conversion and traffic sources, including the search terms visitors used; visits and listing views are different metrics. — [Using Etsy Stats](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015774268-How-to-Use-Etsy-Stats-for-Your-Shop)
- Etsy Ads reports impressions/views, clicks, click rate, attributed orders/revenue, spend and ROAS. Attribution can include purchase of any shop item within 30 days of an ad interaction. Clicks cost money; views do not. — [Review Etsy Ads performance](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034223613-How-to-Review-the-Performance-of-Your-Etsy-Ads)

### Inferences
Proposed operating plan:
- Days 1–14: establish seller and Printify access; inspect account warnings, balances, expired/inactive listings and past orders; export historical metrics before editing; choose initial target country and delivery promise; confirm variant mapping, provider, payment method and order approval settings; build actual unit economics; obtain samples and inspect sizing, print, packaging and wash performance. No paid launch until essential checks pass.
- Days 15–30: relaunch the validated 6–12 designs, complete shop identity and policies, accurate partner details and listing media. Record a baseline per listing. Post original sample/design content on one chosen social channel. If launching in November, use provider-confirmed holiday deadlines; do not promise Christmas arrival from generic estimates.
- Days 31–60: review visits, search phrases and orders weekly. Improve one material variable per experiment (thumbnail, product concept, price or copy) and log dates. When listings and fulfillment are sound and budget is agreed, run a bounded Etsy Ads test on a small number of promising products. Avoid treating a few clicks or one sale as proof.
- Days 61–90: expand successful original design families and sizes only when orders remain profitable after actual advertising, refunds and replacements. Increase budgets gradually and monitor operations. If interest stays weak, revise offer or artwork before adding channels and software.
- Weekly scorecard: visits; orders; shop conversion = orders/visits; revenue; average order value; production plus shipping cost; platform/payment fees; ad spend; refunds/replacements; contribution after advertising; on-time fulfillment; support response backlog; cash available for fulfillment.
- Diagnostic logic: low exposure means examine query relevance and offer; exposure without clicks means examine photo, title and displayed total price; clicks without orders means examine fit, delivery, value and trust; orders without contribution means fix costs/pricing before scaling.
- Budget formula: maximum affordable acquisition cost = pre-ad contribution per order minus required retained contribution. Break-even ROAS = attributed revenue / contribution before advertising, using matching revenue/cost definitions. Positive ROAS alone is not profit. Attribution is not proof of incremental sales.
- Set a cash loss limit before testing; pause when it is reached. Do not prescribe arbitrary universal conversion or ROAS benchmarks. Scale after repeat observed profitable orders and reliable delivery, not after hitting a fixed date.

### Gaps
- Without account data there is no defensible revenue target, advertising budget or conversion forecast.
- Historical returns and actual fulfillment performance are unverified.

## What should the website and repository become?

### Takeaway
Use Etsy and the existing Printify setup as the initial selling and fulfillment path if verified. Build the repository first as the business operating record; add a standalone checkout only when there is a reason and an independent acquisition plan.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy prohibits moving transactions initiated on Etsy off-platform, including encouraging buyers to purchase the shop item elsewhere. — [Off-Platform Transactions policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/off-platform-transactions/1254654515806)

### Inferences
- Immediate repo roadmap: README describing business, channels and current verified state; product catalog schema; original design sources and license records; supplier/variant mapping; actual pricing model; listing drafts and media; launch checklist; support/returns procedures; experiment log; aggregate weekly performance; decision log. Keep credentials and customer personal data out of Git.
- Do not build custom checkout, inventory, shipping or order software before knowing the operational problem it solves. Print-on-demand still requires seller responsibility for support, refunds, cash flow, quality and exceptions.
- If an owned presence is wanted now, a small brand site can explain products and link to Etsy checkout. It must not misrepresent stock, shipping or integration. A future standalone shop needs payments, tax configuration, delivery/returns, privacy/consent, tracking, support, order integration and reliable independent traffic.
- Standalone expansion criteria: validated contribution and products; reliable supplier and exception process; a traffic source outside Etsy; time and budget for operating two channels; a clear measurable benefit outweighing platform and acquisition costs. Etsy-initiated purchases must remain on Etsy.

### Gaps
- The best future platform cannot be selected without seller jurisdiction, payments eligibility, budget, technical preference and acquisition plan.
